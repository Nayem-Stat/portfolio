## 🎯 Professional Summary

Statistician and data scientist with **5+ years of experience** building statistical and machine learning models for real-world data in research, government-funded projects and industry. My methodological work focuses on **regularized and shrinkage estimation** (ridge, lasso, elastic net, ridge-hurdle and zero-inflated count models), **high-dimensional regression** and **simulation-based evaluation of estimators**. It has produced **17 publications**, **8 papers under review** and **23 conference presentations**. I hold the **ASA Data Science Professional (DSP)** certification (2026–2031).

**Key Expertise:**
- Regularized regression and multicollinearity (ridge, lasso, elastic net, shrinkage estimators)
- Count-data modeling: Poisson, negative binomial, zero-inflated and hurdle models
- High-dimensional data analysis, feature screening and variable selection
- Machine learning and explainable AI (tree ensembles, SHAP)
- Monte Carlo simulation, statistical inference and confidence intervals
- Data pipelines, reporting, and monitoring & evaluation (M&E) for funded projects

**Proficient in:** R, Python, SQL, SAS, Stata, SPSS, MATLAB, Power BI, Tableau, AWS, Azure, GCP, Databricks, Snowflake
