#### Symposium on Data Science & Statistics (SDSS 2026)
**Conference:** 2026 Symposium on Data Science & Statistics  
**Date:** April 29, 2026  
**Location:** Milwaukee, Wisconsin  
**Presentation Type:** Contributed Talk

**Title:** "Evaluating Ridge Penalties for High-Dimensional Linear Models Under Multicollinearity"  
**Authors:** Nayem, H., and Kibria, B. M. G.  
**Abstract:** 
Presents a comprehensive evaluation of ridge regression penalty methods for handling multicollinearity in high-dimensional linear models. The work compares various ridge penalty selection criteria including cross-validation, generalized cross-validation, and information criteria to identify optimal penalty parameters.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Ridge penalty selection methodologies
- Comparative performance evaluation
- Practical guidance for practitioners
- Extension to high-dimensional problems

#### ASA Florida Chapter Meeting
**Conference:** 2026 Annual ASA Florida Chapter Meeting  
**Date:** March 7, 2026  
**Location:** Florida International University, Miami, Florida  
**Presentation Type:** Contributed Talk

**Title:** "A Regularized Hurdle Framework for Addressing Multicollinearity in Zero-inflated Count Data"  
**Authors:** Nayem, H., and Kibria, B. G. M.  
**Abstract:**
Introduces a novel regularized hurdle model framework specifically designed for zero-inflated count data under multicollinearity conditions. The approach combines hurdle regression with ridge regularization to simultaneously address zero-inflation and correlated predictors.

**Key Contributions:**
- Novel methodological framework
- Theoretical properties and asymptotic behavior
- Simulation study and empirical validation
- Software implementation in R

#### 2025 Joint Statistical Meeting (JSM 2025)
**Conference:** 2025 Joint Statistical Meeting  
**Date:** August 5, 2025  
**Location:** Nashville, Tennessee  
**Presentation Type:** Contributed Talk

**Title:** "Robustness of OLS, Ridge, Lasso, and Elastic Net in the Presence of Outliers: Simulation and Application"  
**Authors:** Nayem, H., Aziz, S., and Kibria, B.M.G.  
**Abstract:**
Comprehensive simulation study comparing the robustness of ordinary least squares (OLS), ridge, Lasso, and elastic net regression methods when data contains outliers. Evaluates multiple robustness metrics and provides practical recommendations.

**Methodology:**
- Controlled simulation experiments
- Multiple outlier scenarios
- Robustness metrics comparison
- Real-world application examples

**Key Results:**
- Performance rankings under outlier conditions
- Penalty parameter sensitivity analysis
- Practical guidance for method selection

#### Quality and Productivity Research Conference (QPRC 2025)
**Conference:** Quality and Productivity Research Conference  
**Date:** June 16, 2025  
**Location:** University of Washington, Seattle, Washington  
**Presentation Type:** Contributed Talk  
**Award:** Travel Scholarship Winner

**Title:** "A Prior-Information-Based Unbiased Convex Estimator for the Classical Linear Regression Model"  
**Authors:** Nayem, H., and Kibria, B.M.G.  
**Abstract:**
Proposes a novel convex estimator that incorporates prior information into classical linear regression without sacrificing the unbiasedness property. The method is particularly useful when prior beliefs about regression parameters are available from previous studies or expert knowledge.

**Innovation:**
- New theoretical framework
- Bayesian-frequentist hybrid approach
- Asymptotic properties derivation
- Simulation and empirical validation

**Applications:**
- Combines historical data effectively
- Improves estimation in small samples
- Maintains unbiasedness property
