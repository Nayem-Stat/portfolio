# Research Projects

## 🚀 Current & Active Projects

### 1. Artificial Intelligence Applications to Enhance Transportation Safety
**Funding:** Florida Department of Transportation (FDOT)  
**Timeline:** 2025–2026  
**Role:** Graduate Research Assistant  
**Status:** Active

**Description:**
Developing and implementing machine learning models to identify safety patterns and predict transportation safety risks. This project focuses on leveraging AI techniques including deep learning, ensemble methods, and predictive analytics to enhance overall transportation safety on Florida highways.

**Key Deliverables:**
- Predictive models for crash risk assessment
- Safety pattern identification using unsupervised learning
- Decision support systems for transportation agencies
- Publication of findings in peer-reviewed journals

**Technologies:** Python, R, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, AWS

### 2. Evaluation of the Effectiveness of WWD Countermeasures on Florida Freeways and Arterials
**Funding:** Florida Department of Transportation (FDOT)  
**Timeline:** 2025–2026  
**Role:** Graduate Research Assistant  
**Status:** Active

**Description:**
Comprehensive evaluation of Wise Wrong-Way Driving (WWD) detection and prevention countermeasures on Florida highways. This study assesses the effectiveness of various technological and physical interventions in reducing wrong-way driving incidents and their associated crashes.

**Key Deliverables:**
- Effectiveness evaluation reports
- Cost-benefit analysis of countermeasures
- Recommendations for best practices
- Conference and journal publications

**Technologies:** Stata, R, GIS analysis, Statistical modeling
