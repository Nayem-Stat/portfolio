## 🧭 How I Work

- **Data preparation:** cleaning, integration and pipelines for multi-source data
- **Modeling:** GLMs, count models, regularized regression and machine learning, chosen to fit the data and the question
- **Evaluation:** cross-validation, Monte Carlo simulation and sensitivity analysis
- **Communication:** peer-reviewed papers, technical reports for funding agencies and conference talks
